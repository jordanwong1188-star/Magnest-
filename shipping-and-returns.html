Shipping & returns

Clear information for your MagnaNest order.

Where we ship

MagnaNest ships to Canada and the United States. Prices are shown in US dollars, and payments are completed through Stripe’s hosted checkout.

Choose your shelf quantity in the storefront before continuing to checkout. Bundle quantities and prices are fixed in Stripe, so your order matches the set you selected.

Prices and shipping

One shelf is US$24.95. Two shelves are US$42.42, saving US$7.48 compared with two singles. Three shelves are US$56.14, saving US$18.71 compared with three singles.

The advertised offer includes free shipping on orders of two or more shelves. Shipping for one shelf is US$2.99, making the single-shelf order total US$27.94 before any separately applicable taxes. Shipping is free on orders of two or more shelves.

Delivery information

We ship to Canada and the United States. A specific delivery date is not promised on this page. Processing, destination and carrier availability can affect delivery.

Keep your Stripe order confirmation for payment and order reference. If you need help with your order, use the seller contact information provided with your purchase.

The 30-day guarantee

MagnaNest comes with a 30-day guarantee. Keep your order confirmation and request return or replacement guidance before sending an item back.

If your item arrives damaged or incorrect, keep the packaging and take clear photographs so the issue can be reviewed. Your statutory consumer rights are not affected.

Choose a compatible surface

The shelf needs a flat, magnetic steel surface. Some stainless-look refrigerator panels are not magnetic. Test your chosen spot with a household magnet first. Pictured jars, plants and other accessories are not included.
