GOOD TO KNOW

A few little
details.

Will it work on my refrigerator?+

MagnaNest needs a flat, magnetic steel surface. Some stainless-look refrigerators are not magnetic. Test the side panel with a household magnet before ordering.

Do I need tools or adhesive?+

No. The shelf attaches magnetically, with no screws, wall anchors or sticky pads.

Can I move it after installing it?+

Yes. Remove the stored items first, lift the shelf away and place it in a new position.

What should I keep on it?+

Lightweight everyday essentials such as spices, snacks, coffee supplies, wraps and small containers. Distribute weight evenly and keep your essentials lightweight.
