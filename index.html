NEED TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Will MagnaNest work on my refrigerator?+

MagnaNest is designed for flat, magnetic steel surfaces. Some stainless-look refrigerators are not magnetic, so test your fridge with a household magnet before ordering.

Do I need tools or adhesive?+

No. MagnaNest attaches magnetically, so there are no screws, wall anchors, or sticky pads required for normal installation.

Can I move it after installing it?+

Yes. Remove stored items first, lift the shelf away, and place it in a new position.

What should I store on it?+

Use it for everyday lightweight kitchen essentials such as spices, snacks, coffee supplies, wraps, and small containers. Distribute weight evenly.
