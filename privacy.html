Privacy

How this MagnaNest storefront handles information while you browse.

Your cart stays on your device

The storefront uses your browser’s local storage to remember the number of shelves in your cart and your selected bundle. Session storage records which browsing and bundle promotions have already been shown, so they are not repeatedly displayed in the same session.

If browser storage is unavailable, the page can keep these settings in memory while it remains open. Clearing this site’s browser data removes saved selections.

Checkout and order information

Payments are completed on Stripe’s hosted checkout. Stripe collects the payment, email and shipping information required to process your purchase. Card details are handled by Stripe rather than stored by this static storefront.

Order information is used to handle payment, fulfillment, delivery, support and refunds. Supplier fulfillment is managed separately from this website. Read Stripe’s privacy policy for its services.

This storefront does not include an email signup form, advertising pixels or analytics scripts.

Website delivery and fonts

The site requests fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to load them. The hosting provider also receives the requests needed to deliver the website and may process technical information for security and operation.

Read Google’s privacy policy and Cloudflare’s privacy policy for their respective services.

Your choices

You can clear this website’s browser data to remove saved cart and bundle selections. For a question about an order or the information needed to fulfill it, use the seller contact details provided with your purchase.

Marketing tools are not included in this storefront. If they are added later, the privacy information and any required consent choices will be updated.
