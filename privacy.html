Privacy

How this MagnaNest storefront handles information while you browse.

Your bag stays on your device.

The storefront uses your browser’s local storage to remember the number of shelves in your bag and your selected bundle. Session storage records which browsing and bundle promotions have already been shown, so they are not repeatedly displayed in the same session.

If browser storage is unavailable, the page can keep these settings in memory while it remains open. Clearing this site’s browser data removes saved selections.

Browsing and payments.

This storefront does not currently include an email signup form, advertising pixels or analytics scripts. It does not collect card details or accept payments. Payment checkout will be connected separately before ordering opens, with the relevant provider information available before payment.

Website delivery and fonts.

The site requests fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to load them. The hosting provider also receives the requests needed to deliver the website and may process technical information for security and operation.

Read Google’s privacy policy and Cloudflare’s privacy policy for their respective services.

Before ordering opens.

Customer support details and privacy information covering customer orders, fulfillment partners and any future marketing tools will be published before those services begin collecting information.
